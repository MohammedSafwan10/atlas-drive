# Terrain material sources

All terrain maps are 1K CC0 PBR scans from Poly Haven:

- `forest_*` — [Forest Ground 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/forrest_ground_01)
- `dirt_*` — [Dirt](https://polyhaven.com/a/dirt)
- `gravel_*` — [Gravel](https://polyhaven.com/a/gravel)

Each material includes a diffuse map, OpenGL normal map, and roughness map.
